How an engagement runs.

Short, senior and pointed at one question. The shape flexes with the problem; the sequence doesn't.

  1. 01

    Scoping & framing

    We define the precise question, agree what success looks like, and identify the sources and stakeholders that matter.

  2. 02

    Research & analysis

    Primary research is core to how we work: interviews, market data and competitor mapping, tested against your internal view.

  3. 03

    Synthesis

    We distil the findings into clear commercial conclusions, challenge assumptions, and say what actually matters.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Findings presented to leadership as a written report or working session, with recommendations you can act on.

Every engagement is scoped to the decision it serves, with a fixed fee agreed before work starts.

Decisions we've helped make.

Three engagements, with clients anonymised by sector and engagement type.

EngagementThe decisionWhat changed
MobilityMarket-entry assessmentA global Tier-1 automotive supplier

Whether to invest in qualifying a new engineered-polymer platform for electric-vehicle applications, with internal views split on which applications to pursue, how long qualification would take, and whether competitors were too far ahead.

We sized demand by application, mapped competitors and OEM qualification timelines, and stress-tested the business case under three scenarios. The recommendation: a focused entry into two priority applications rather than a broad launch. Leadership approved the narrower plan and redirected capital and technical resources to it.

 $45Maddressable annual opportunity identified, with a ~30% shorter projected path to first revenue
Specialty ChemicalsPortfolio and positioning reviewA specialty chemicals producer

Growth had flattened across a broad portfolio. Leadership needed to know which segments justified investment, which were diluting focus, and where the company had a defensible position.

We tested long-held assumptions against customer interviews and market evidence, then ranked each segment on growth, margin and competitive strength. The commercial team shifted sales and marketing resources toward the strongest segments and began phasing down the weakest.

 ~60%of commercial effort refocused on the three highest-potential segments, targeting a 3–5 point margin improvement
Electrical & ElectronicsRapid strategic decision supportA mid-market electronics manufacturer

A board decision was due within weeks: commit to a new product direction or hold course, with no time for a conventional study.

We delivered a focused, board-ready recommendation covering demand, competitive response, supply-chain exposure and risk, written for decision-makers, not analysts. The board approved a revised launch sequence that changed the company's priorities and gave it a clear rationale to proceed.
