ProjektDie EntscheidungWas sich geändert hat
MobilityMarket-entry assessmentA global Tier-1 automotive supplier

Whether to invest in qualifying a new engineered-polymer platform for electric-vehicle applications, with internal views split on which applications to pursue, how long qualification would take, and whether competitors were too far ahead.

We sized demand by application, mapped competitors and OEM qualification timelines, and stress-tested the business case under three scenarios. The recommendation: a focused entry into two priority applications rather than a broad launch. Leadership approved the narrower plan and redirected capital and technical resources to it.

 $45Maddressable annual opportunity identified, with a ~30% shorter projected path to first revenue
