A recent engagement

EngagementThe decisionWhat changed
Specialty ChemicalsPortfolio and positioning reviewA specialty chemicals producer

Growth had flattened across a broad portfolio. Leadership needed to know which segments justified investment, which were diluting focus, and where the company had a defensible position.

We tested long-held assumptions against customer interviews and market evidence, then ranked each segment on growth, margin and competitive strength. The commercial team shifted sales and marketing resources toward the strongest segments and began phasing down the weakest.

 ~60%of commercial effort refocused on the three highest-potential segments, targeting a 3–5 point margin improvement

How an engagement runs.

  1. 01

    Scoping & framing

    We define the precise question, agree what success looks like, and identify the sources and stakeholders that matter.

  2. 02

    Research & analysis

    Primary research is core to how we work: interviews, market data and competitor mapping, tested against your internal view.

  3. 03

    Synthesis

    We distil the findings into clear commercial conclusions, challenge assumptions, and say what actually matters.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Findings presented to leadership as a written report or working session, with recommendations you can act on.

Every engagement is scoped to the decision it serves, with a fixed fee agreed before work starts.
